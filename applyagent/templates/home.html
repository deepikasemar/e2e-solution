What the agent will and will not do

It does

  • Extract skills, education, projects and contact lines from PDF, DOCX or TXT.
  • Score jobs as skill overlap plus cosine similarity plus your location, role and work-type preferences.
  • Show matching skills and missing skills, including a gap such as Docker.
  • Write a summary and a cover letter, then flag skills that are not on the profile.
  • Prefill an application packet after you approve it, and keep a daily limit.

It does not

  • Send an application to a company, or click through a portal for you.
  • Use your name, gender, age or phone number to rank jobs.
  • Invent employers, dates or tools so the letter sounds stronger.
  • Promise an interview. The evaluation page shows measured scores, not a placement claim.
